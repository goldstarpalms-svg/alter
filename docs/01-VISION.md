# 01 — Why ALTER exists

Most software is a tool *you use*. ALTER is different: it's a **presence** — a copy of *you* that lives on the thing you already carry (your phone), and that you can summon into the room whenever you want.

It is not a cloud assistant that reports to a server. It is **yours**, local-first, with no account and no backend. It knows the world (you can plug in any LLM with one free API key) and it knows *you* (persistent on-device memory). It has a face that blinks and talks, and it can literally float in your room over your live camera.

The thesis: a personal AI shouldn't be a corporate dashboard. It should feel like a **self** — always on, always learning, fully sovereign over your own data.

Built by a mechanical engineering student who codes and builds websites, on a $50 budget, with no server, to prove that a device-scale AI self is possible today.
